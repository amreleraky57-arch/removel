Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026. We take privacy seriously — here's everything you need to know.

The Short Version

Your images never leave your device. ClearFrame AI processes everything locally in your browser using standard web APIs. We have no server-side image processing, no file storage, and no access to your photos whatsoever.

1. Information We Collect

Images and Files

We do not collect, receive, or store any images you process with ClearFrame AI. All image processing occurs entirely within your browser's JavaScript sandbox. Images are loaded into HTML5 Canvas elements on your device and processed there. Nothing is transmitted over the network.

Usage Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics such as page views, browser type, operating system, and geographic region (country-level only). This data contains no personally identifiable information and is used solely to improve the product.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for site functionality. We do not use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking cookies.

2. How We Use Information

The limited anonymous data we collect is used to:

  • Understand how people use the tool so we can improve it
  • Monitor site performance and uptime
  • Detect and prevent abuse

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

ClearFrame AI loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome CDN. These services may set their own cookies and have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them if you have concerns.

We do not use third-party advertising networks that track users across sites.

4. Data Security

Because we do not receive or store your images or personal data, there is nothing to breach from our end. The security of your images depends on your local device security.

5. Children's Privacy

ClearFrame AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of ClearFrame AI after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page.
