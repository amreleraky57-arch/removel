Our Story

Built for People Who Value Privacy

ClearFrame AI was created because people deserve tools that respect their data — not exploit it.

Why We Built This

Most online image editors require you to upload your photos to remote servers. That means your private images — personal photos, client work, confidential documents — sit on someone else's computer. We think that's a problem.

ClearFrame AI is different. We built the entire processing pipeline to run directly in your browser. The WebGL-accelerated canvas API handles everything locally. Your images never leave your device.

The Technology

Our watermark removal engine uses an alpha-blending reversal algorithm combined with a diffusion-based inpainting system. When you click "Remove," the engine:

  • Analyzes the watermark's transparency pattern (alpha channel)
  • Applies reverse alpha-blending mathematics to recover the original pixel values
  • Uses a multi-pass diffusion inpainting algorithm to fill any remaining gaps
  • Applies subtle noise to prevent visible reconstruction artifacts

For the manual brush mode, the Telea inpainting algorithm reconstructs masked regions based on surrounding pixel context.

Our Principles

Free, forever. We believe basic image editing tools should be universally accessible. ClearFrame AI is and will remain free to use with no limits.

Privacy by default. We will never add server-side processing that requires uploading your images. If we can't do it in the browser, we won't do it.

Honest about limitations. Watermark removal is a hard problem. Results vary by image. We won't promise perfect results on every image — but we'll keep making the engine better.
